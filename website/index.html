What it does

Everything a draft needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

No accounts, no dashboards, no clutter. Just the handful of tools that help you stay inside the story.

Paragraph focus

Dim everything except the paragraph you’re writing. It follows your cursor as you type and never touches your text.

A writing desk that behaves

Browse your folder, drag files into place, color and pin what matters, rename, and search every subfolder. Switch files without leaving the window.

Your outline, your words

Headings become an outline you can name Chapters, Scenes, or Episodes. Jump anywhere with a click.

A second file beside your draft

Open a character sheet or a map of the world next to your chapter, read it cleanly, and edit it in place when you need to.

Gentle prose suggestions

Light strikethroughs mark words you might cut. Sentence coloring shows nouns and verbs. Both run on your Mac and change nothing until you say so.

A toolbar that gets out of the way

It slides in when you reach for it and disappears when you don’t. Put it on the top or the side, keep it visible, or turn it off.

No lock-in

Your writing is just files.

Sable saves ordinary Markdown. Open your chapters in any editor, keep them in a folder you control, and take them anywhere.

  • Plain text. Bold, italics, headings and quotes are ordinary Markdown marks. Nothing proprietary, nothing hidden.
  • Your cloud, your rules. Keep the folder in iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive and your service handles sync.
  • Display only. Themes, focus, colors and suggestions never change what’s saved.

Download and unzip

Get Sable-Markdown-Writer.zip from the latest release and double-click it.

Move to Applications

Drag Sable Markdown Writer.app into your Applications folder.

Open it

Choose a writing folder when asked, or create a new one. Then start on page one. The guide covers shortcuts and tips.

A note on first launch. Sable isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so macOS may block it the first time. If you trust the download, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway. Updates later arrive inside the app, verified with the project’s signing key.

Questions

Good to know

Is Sable really free?

Yes. Sable is open source under the MIT license, with no account, subscription, or paid tier.

Is Sable a good Markdown editor for writing a novel?

It is built for exactly that. Your headings become a chapter outline, paragraph focus keeps you inside one passage, a second file can sit beside your draft for notes, and every chapter stays an ordinary Markdown file. It is a young app, and it does not yet export to PDF or ebooks.

Does it sync between devices?

Sable does not run a cloud service. Keep your writing folder in iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive and that service syncs the files. Let it finish syncing before you edit the same file elsewhere.

Does Sable use AI?

No. Prose suggestions come from a word list you control, and sentence coloring uses Apple’s Natural Language tools on your Mac. Nothing you write is sent anywhere.

What files can it open?

Markdown and text files: .md, .markdown, and .txt. Files stay exactly as you wrote them.

Can I keep using other apps on the same files?

Yes. Sable saves plain Markdown in ordinary folders, so any other editor can open the same files. Themes, focus, colors, and suggestions are display only and never change what is saved.

Is there a Windows, iPad, or iPhone version?

Sable is Mac-only for now. An iPad edition, Fiction Projects, and PDF and ebook export are on the roadmap, not in the app today.

Why does macOS say I have to choose Open Anyway?

The app is signed for updates but not notarized by Apple, which needs a paid developer membership. It is a one-time approval in System Settings, Privacy & Security, and you can read every line of the source.
