Sable Markdown Writer 0.6.1 refines the writing desk and toolbar and fixes file switching.

- The Writing Desk now browses nested folders. Clicking a Markdown file switches the active document in the same window, with macOS save, discard, and cancel choices when needed.
- Automatic writing tabs are disabled. Control-click a file to open it beside your draft instead. The parallel pane begins in a clean formatted view and can be edited and saved in place.
- New writers begin with a blank document and choose a writing folder, with an optional editable Markdown guide and cloud-folder recommendations.
- Five themes, fiction-oriented font presets, custom fonts, zoom, optional pinch-to-zoom, paragraph focus, and a top-edge toolbar make the page easier to shape around your work.
- The writing desk can be shown with a two-finger horizontal trackpad gesture. The sentence-structure color picker no longer changes or crashes the Markdown editor.

- Fixes: switching files no longer closes and reopens the window or leaves full screen. The top toolbar and the writing desk now slide in and out smoothly.
- The writing desk is calmer: Files and Outline are separate tabs, extensions are hidden by default, and the list can be sorted, resized, and tuned (icons, dates, word counts, compact rows).
- Folders can be colored, given your own color labels, pinned, filtered, focused, searched (across every subfolder), dragged into each other, renamed, and moved to the Trash with undo. Arrow keys work in the list.
- The toolbar can sit on the top, left, or right; auto-hide, always show, or turn off (⌥⌘T).
- Settings can regenerate the Sable guide. The app no longer opens a file picker at launch; it reopens your last document.

- New **Chalk** theme (graphite slate with a soft edge gradient), and a darker bottom bar in Graphite, Midnight, and Chalk.
- Paragraph focus now fades text gradually with distance from what you're writing. The classic even dimming is still available in Writing Style.
- The top toolbar is compact again, and every tool names itself and its shortcut when you rest the pointer on it.
- **Fiction Projects (first steps):** create a project with a ready-made folder structure, see only the project once you're inside it, add chapters, characters, locations, and world notes from templates, and convert a project back to a regular folder at any time. Characters, locations, and world notes can float over the page as cards that snap to a corner, pin open or shrink to a tab, and carry a picture. Everything stays ordinary Markdown.
- A document open beside your draft now scrolls and zooms on its own; scaling one no longer resizes the other.
- Fiction Projects: the Manuscript folder is always pinned and red by default, a visible **Leave** button exits a project, projects are listed apart from ordinary folders, and character cards can be resized freely and have their portraits cropped.
- Every new Fiction Project starts with a **Start Here** guide covering how projects work and how to customize them.
- **Categories** (School, Work, Hobbies…) let you group folders in the writing folder by dragging them onto a heading, with collapse, color, and reordering.
- A **Manuscript** tab in Fiction Projects shows your total word count, an optional goal with progress, and every chapter with its length. Drag chapters to rearrange them; the order is saved in the project without renaming or changing any file.
- You can select several files and folders at once (⌘-click, ⇧-click, ⇧-arrows, ⌘A) and move them to the Trash together, with one Undo. Move to Trash now works on the file you have open, too.
- Chapters can be dragged into a new order in the Files tab's Manuscript folder as well as in the Manuscript tab.
- **Scene tags:** tag the location and characters in a chapter and see them as a quiet strip of chips while you write. Click a chip to open its card without leaving the page. Choose from your cards, or create one for a name that doesn't have one yet. The strip fades while you type and can sit at the bottom, in a corner, or be turned off.
- A **+** on the Manuscript, Characters, Locations, and World folders adds a chapter, character, location, or world note.
- **Scroll past the end** (Writing Style → Scrolling): room to scroll so the line you're writing can rest in the middle of the window, or an option that keeps it centered as you type.
- Scene-tag chips take the color of their card, and in paragraph focus the strip and collapsed card tabs dim until you point at them.
- Creating a character or location no longer opens its card automatically.

This remains a Mac prototype. iCloud conflict behavior, large-manuscript performance, Fiction Projects, PDF export, and ebook export are still ahead.
